Why an ENT surgeon

A nose has to work as well as it looks.

Many U.S. rhinoplasty specialists are facial plastic surgeons trained in ENT. Dr. Dilber works the same way: he plans the outside shape and the airway inside it together, so a smaller nose doesn't leave you breathing worse.

  • Breathing fixed in the same surgery

    Deviated septum, enlarged turbinates and collapsing valves are corrected while he reshapes the nose.

  • Structure that lasts

    Tip support and septal extension grafts keep the result stable for years, not just at the 3-month photo.

  • Ultrasonic bone shaping

    Piezo instruments refine the bridge precisely where appropriate, with less trauma to soft tissue.

Revision rhinoplasty

Unhappy with a previous nose job?

Revision is a large part of Dr. Dilber's practice, including second and third revisions. He reviews your old operation notes and current photos first, then rebuilds what was lost.

Get a revision assessment

What revision usually involves

  • Rebuilding support with cartilage from the septum, ear or rib
  • Correcting a pinched, collapsed or over-reduced tip
  • Fixing breathing problems left by the first surgery
  • Releasing scar tissue and restoring a smooth bridge

Revision is quoted individually after a photo review.

    Who is Dr. Muhammet Dilber?

    Assoc. Prof. Dr. Muhammet Dilber is an ear, nose and throat (ENT) surgeon in Istanbul, Türkiye, whose practice is focused on rhinoplasty.     Why choose an ENT surgeon for rhinoplasty?

    ENT (otorhinolaryngology) surgeons are trained in both the outside shape of the nose and the airway inside it. Dr. Muhammet Dilber can correct a deviated septum, enlarged turbinates or weak valves in the same operation as the cosmetic change.

    What procedures does Dr. Muhammet Dilber perform?

    Primary rhinoplasty, revision rhinoplasty (including second and third revisions), septorhinoplasty for breathing problems, and ultrasonic (piezo) rhinoplasty. Septum, turbinate and nasal valve problems can be corrected in the same operation.
